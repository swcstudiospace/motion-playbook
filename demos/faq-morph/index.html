03 · FAQ morph

Open one, the grid makes room.

Click a question. The others fade out and the card grows to fill the whole grid. Click anywhere outside it, click the question again, or press Escape to put everything back.

Bento grid

01

Why separate the cell from the card?

The cell is the grid item and owns the scroll reveal. The card inside it is free for the morph. If both animated the same element's transform, anime.js would replace one animation with the other.

02

What is FLIP?

First, Last, Invert, Play. Measure the card, change the layout, measure again, then animate from the old box to the new one with transform and size.

03

Does the page jump?

No. The grid keeps its height while one card is open, so nothing below it moves and scroll-linked scenes keep their timing.

04

Is it accessible?

It's a native details element, so the question is a real button. Hidden cards leave the tab order, Escape closes and returns focus, and reduced motion swaps the morph for an instant change.

Stacked list

01

Same code, one column?

Yes. Only the CSS differs. The open card takes the full grid area whatever the layout.

02

How long does it take?

260 ms to fade the others out, 560 ms for the card to settle, with the answer starting 260 ms in. Closing is a little quicker than opening.

03

What if I double-click?

Clicks are ignored while a morph is running, so the card can't get stuck halfway.
